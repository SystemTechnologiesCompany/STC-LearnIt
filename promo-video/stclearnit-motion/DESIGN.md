# STC Learnit Motion Design Reference

## Overview

The live STC Learnit homepage uses an open, student-friendly layout with generous off-white space, charcoal-green typography, lime accents, rounded study cards, and a clear sequence from choosing a subject to advancing through a learning path. The film starts in the Basic identity, travels through the site’s real faculty, lesson, level, and leaderboard interfaces, then reveals the two premium theme palettes. DM Sans and Manrope are the captured brand typefaces.

## Typography

- **Manrope:** headlines and kinetic display copy; heavy weights, tight tracking.
- **DM Sans:** interface labels, supporting copy, and end-card details.
- **Hierarchy:** one large idea per frame, 16px minimum for small labels, high contrast throughout.

## Elevation

Use layered UI panels with rounded corners, fine borders, soft glows, and restrained depth. The camera can tilt captured site screens in 3D, but keep the key interface readable. Galaxy and Sakura use localized color glows and small particles, not full-frame noisy textures.

## Components

- **Learning path preview:** compact course card with progress, lesson nodes, XP, and streak markers.
- **Faculty chooser:** rounded, grid-based subject cards.
- **Learn / Play / Advance cards:** illustrated cards with the captured book, controller, and trophy assets.
- **Level progression:** linked level nodes leading into an exam and level-up moment.
- **Leaderboard podium:** three podium columns with a prominent first place and restrained gold glow.
- **Premium theme cards:** Galaxy and Sakura theme surfaces shown side by side before the final brand lockup.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use the captured site screens and the actual STC head logo.
- Make the Basic → Galaxy → Sakura progression unmistakable.
- Use smooth camera pushes, match cuts, 3D tilts, and controlled morphs.
- Let the supplied voice-over set the edit duration.

### Don'ts

- Do not show fabricated rankings, XP totals, or identifiable student accounts.
- Do not put text over the busiest part of a captured interface.
- Do not overfill the frame with particles or let them obscure the narration's key message.
- Do not speed up or remove words from the supplied recording to force a shorter runtime.
